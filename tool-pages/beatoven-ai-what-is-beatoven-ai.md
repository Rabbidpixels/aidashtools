# What is Beatoven.ai?

Beatoven.ai creates custom background music that adapts to your video's emotional pacing—analyzing your content and generating music that intensifies during exciting moments, mellows during calm scenes, and matches the emotional arc of your story. Rather than generic background tracks, Beatoven produces contextually aware music specifically scored to your content. For video creators tired of music that doesn't quite fit or requires tedious editing to sync, Beatoven offers genuinely smart music generation that understands narrative flow.

## The Context-Aware Approach

Most AI music generators create tracks based on genre or mood descriptions without understanding what they'll accompany. Beatoven takes different approach: upload your video or specify duration and emotional beats, and it generates music following that structure. Need energetic music for the first 30 seconds, contemplative for the middle section, triumphant for the conclusion? Beatoven composes music matching that progression.

This context awareness addresses real frustration: finding or creating music that fits content emotionally and temporally. Generic tracks require extensive editing to sync properly. Beatoven generates music already structured for your specific content needs.
