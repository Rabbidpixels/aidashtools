# What is Soundraw?

Soundraw positions itself as the customizable AI music generator—giving creators more hands-on control than fully automated tools while remaining far simpler than traditional music production. Instead of describing what you want and hoping AI interprets correctly, you actively shape the music through intuitive controls adjusting mood, tempo, instruments, and structure. For content creators who want music that fits precisely without musical training or expensive production, Soundraw hits a practical sweet spot between automation and customization.

## The Customization Philosophy

Most AI music generators work like vending machines—insert prompt, receive music, hope it's what you needed. Soundraw adds interactive refinement. Generate a track, then adjust energy levels at specific moments, swap instruments, modify tempo, restructure sections, or fine-tune mood progression. The music responds to your adjustments in real-time, letting you sculpt results toward your vision.

This hands-on approach serves creators who know what they need but can't produce it themselves. You're directing the AI rather than hoping it guesses correctly, gaining creative control without requiring production expertise.

## The Limitations

**Instrumental Only**
Soundraw doesn't generate vocals or lyrics. If your project needs singing, other tools provide those capabilities.

**Electronic/Modern Focus**
While covering multiple genres, Soundraw leans toward contemporary electronic and pop styles. Orchestral, classical, or acoustic genres may have less depth.

**Genre Conventions**
The music follows expected genre patterns rather than pushing boundaries. This consistency ensures professional results but limits experimental or truly unique outcomes.

**Not for Featured Music**
Soundraw creates background and production music excellently. If music is your primary content rather than supporting element, song-generation tools like Suno offer better options.

**Subscription Requirement**
While reasonable for regular users, subscription costs matter for casual or occasional music needs. No per-track purchase options exist.
