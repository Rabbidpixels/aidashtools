# What is AIVA?

AIVA (Artificial Intelligence Virtual Artist) is the professional composer's AI tool—designed specifically for creating orchestral, cinematic, and emotional music with actual musical sophistication. While many AI music generators lean heavily electronic or experimental, AIVA focuses on the traditional: film scores, game soundtracks, classical compositions, and production music that sounds like it came from trained composers rather than algorithms. For filmmakers, game developers, and projects requiring dramatic, emotionally resonant music, AIVA offers a specialized solution that understands musical tradition alongside AI capability.

## The Classical AI Composer

AIVA was trained on thousands of classical compositions from history's great composers—Bach, Beethoven, Mozart, and beyond. This classical foundation means AIVA understands musical theory, orchestration principles, and compositional techniques that create emotionally powerful music. It's not generating random notes hoping they sound good; it's applying centuries of compositional knowledge through AI models.

The result is music that respects musical tradition. Harmonic progressions make sense. Orchestration sounds natural. Emotional arcs build and resolve appropriately. For users wanting AI music that doesn't sound obviously synthetic or amateurish, AIVA delivers professional compositional quality.

## What AIVA Creates

**Cinematic Orchestral Music**
Epic film scores, dramatic underscores, emotional themes—AIVA excels at big, sweeping orchestral compositions. Need a hero's theme? Tragic backstory music? Climactic battle soundtrack? AIVA handles these with genuine musical understanding.

**Game Soundtracks**
Background music and thematic compositions for games, from ambient exploration tracks to intense combat music. The ability to generate variations maintaining thematic consistency helps create cohesive game soundscapes.

**Emotional Piano Compositions**
Beyond full orchestra, AIVA creates beautiful solo piano pieces ranging from introspective melancholy to uplifting hope. These work perfectly for emotional film moments or standalone musical pieces.

**Thematic Development**
AIVA can develop musical themes—starting with motifs and expanding them through variations, developments, and orchestral treatments. This compositional sophistication enables creating cohesive scores rather than disconnected tracks.

**Multiple Genre Styles**
While specializing in classical and cinematic, AIVA handles jazz, pop, rock, and other genres, though with varying sophistication. The classical training translates reasonably well to other structured musical forms.

## The Professional Approach

AIVA positions itself for professional use. The interface offers more control than casual music generators—tempo, key, instrumentation, mood, and structural guidance. Users can influence compositions rather than just hoping random generation produces something usable.

The platform provides downloadable MIDI files and audio stems, enabling further editing, mixing, or integration into larger productions. For professionals who view AI as starting point requiring refinement, this flexibility matters enormously.

Licensing is clearly defined with different tiers for personal, indie, and professional use. AIVA addresses the legal ambiguity plaguing AI music by providing explicit usage rights appropriate to subscription levels.
