# What is Synthesia?

Synthesia is the AI video platform that puts digital humans—eerily realistic avatars—front and center in your videos, speaking your script in dozens of languages without you ever appearing on camera or hiring presenters. For corporate training, e-learning, product explainers, or any content requiring a human presenter, Synthesia offers a genuinely different approach: type your script, choose an avatar, and generate polished presenter videos without filming anyone.

## The Avatar Revolution

Traditional presenter videos require on-camera talent, filming equipment, good lighting, audio recording, and considerable time. Reshoot when something changes? Schedule everyone again, set up equipment, record new takes. Translate for different markets? Hire multilingual presenters or deal with subtitles and dubbing.

Synthesia eliminates all of that. The avatars—AI-generated digital humans—deliver your content with realistic lip-sync, natural gestures, and professional presence. Need the same video in fifteen languages? Generate fifteen versions from one script. Content needs updating? Edit the script and regenerate. No cameras, studios, or scheduling.

## How It Works

**Script-Based Generation**
Write or paste your script. Select an avatar from Synthesia's library—professional presenters of various ages, appearances, and styles. Choose voice, language, and video settings. Hit generate, and minutes later you have a complete presenter video.

The avatars aren't cartoon characters—they're photorealistic digital humans that, while not perfectly indistinguishable from real people, are convincingly professional. Viewers understand they're watching AI, but the quality maintains engagement and credibility.

**Custom Avatars**
For branding consistency or personal representation, Synthesia can create custom avatars. Film yourself once (following their guidelines), and they generate a digital version that can present any future content without additional filming. This is powerful for executives, trainers, or brand personalities needing scalable presence.

**Multilingual Capabilities**
The same avatar can speak 120+ languages with appropriate lip-sync and natural intonation. Creating globally-distributed content no longer requires polyglot presenters or expensive localization.

**Screen Recording and Slides**
Combine avatar presenters with screen recordings, slides, or other visuals. The avatars aren't just floating heads—they present alongside content like real instructors or explainers.

## The Primary Use Cases

**Corporate Training and E-Learning**
This is Synthesia's sweet spot. Employee onboarding, compliance training, skills development, and educational content benefit enormously from presenter-led videos. Synthesia makes creating and updating training content dramatically more efficient and cost-effective.

Traditional training video production is expensive enough that content often becomes outdated before being refreshed. Synthesia's economics enable keeping training current, versioning for different departments, and personalizing for different audiences.

**Product Explainers and Demos**
Software companies, B2B vendors, and service providers create walkthrough videos explaining products or services. The professional presenter elevates credibility beyond text-and-screenshots while avoiding the complexity of filming.

**Internal Communications**
Executive messages, company updates, or announcements delivered by avatar versions of leadership. The personal touch of seeing someone speak beats reading emails, without requiring executives to film constantly.

**Sales and Marketing**
Personalized outreach at scale—same core message delivered by consistent presenter but customizable for different prospects, industries, or use cases.

**Accessibility and Translation**
Organizations with global audiences create content once, then generate localized versions automatically. This dramatically reduces barriers to serving international markets or multilingual workforces.

## The Advantages

**Consistency and Quality**
Every video maintains professional quality without worrying about lighting, audio, or presenter performance variations. The avatar is always polished, always on-message, never has bad days.

**Speed and Scalability**
Produce ten videos faster than traditionally filming one. Update content in minutes rather than rescheduling production. Scale to languages and variations that would be practically impossible otherwise.

**Cost Efficiency**
For organizations producing regular training or explainer content, Synthesia's subscription costs are minimal compared to traditional video production expenses—especially when factoring in updates and translations.

**Overcome Camera Shyness**
Subject matter experts who hate being on camera can still have presenter presence. Their expertise gets delivered professionally without personal filming discomfort.

## The Limitations and Concerns

**The Uncanny Factor**
While impressive, Synthesia avatars aren't perfectly human. Viewers can tell they're watching AI-generated presenters. For some contexts this doesn't matter; for others it affects credibility or engagement.

**Limited Emotional Range**
Avatars deliver content professionally but lack the charisma, humor, and emotional connection exceptional human presenters provide. They're competent, rarely compelling.

**Not for Entertainment**
Synthesia serves corporate, educational, and informational needs. For marketing content requiring creative flair or entertainment value, human presenters usually work better.

**Ethical Considerations**
Synthetic human presenters raise questions about authenticity and disclosure. Should viewers be told they're watching AI? What about deepfake concerns? Responsible use requires thought about disclosure and appropriate contexts.

**Subscription Costs**
While economical for organizations producing regular content, individual or occasional use faces high per-minute costs. It's priced for business use, not casual creators.

**B2B Companies**
Software vendors, service providers, and business-focused brands creating product explainers, customer education, or sales enablement content.

## Who Should Look Elsewhere

**Consumer Brands and Entertainment**
Content where authenticity, personality, and emotional connection drive effectiveness. Human presenters deliver engagement avatars can't match for consumer-facing content.

**Individual Creators and Hobbyists**
Pricing and feature sets target organizations, not individuals. Personal YouTube creators or hobbyists find other tools more suitable.

**Artistic or Creative Projects**
Synthesia optimizes for professional competence, not creative distinctiveness. Projects requiring unique visual style or artistic direction need different approaches.

## The Ethics and Responsibility

Synthesia takes responsible AI seriously, implementing safeguards against misuse. You can't create avatars of real people without consent. Content moderation prevents generating harmful material. But broader questions remain about synthetic media, authenticity, and disclosure.

Users bear responsibility for ethical deployment—being transparent about AI generation when appropriate, considering viewer trust, and using the technology for legitimate purposes rather than deception.

## The Bottom Line

Synthesia solves specific, valuable problems for organizations producing informational video content at scale. For training, education, corporate communications, and B2B explainers, it transforms economics and efficiency in ways that genuinely matter.

It's not replacing human presenters in contexts where authentic connection matters. It's eliminating barriers to having presenter videos in contexts where professional competence is sufficient and traditional production is prohibitively expensive or slow.

If you're an organization that should be creating more training videos, product explainers, or educational content but production costs and complexity make it impractical, Synthesia might transform what's possible. If you're creating content where human authenticity and emotional connection drive success, real presenters remain irreplaceable.

Understanding which scenario describes your needs determines whether Synthesia's capabilities matter or whether they're solving problems you don't actually have.
